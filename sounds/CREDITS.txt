Sound effects for Manhunt.

Many are adapted from Kenney's free audio packs (Interface Sounds, Digital Audio, Impact Sounds,
Sci-fi Sounds), released under CC0 1.0 (public domain): https://kenney.nl/assets
The rest (whistle, siren, sonar ping, jingles, breathing, smoke, flare, shimmer) are synthesised
by tools/build-sounds.py and are free to use.
